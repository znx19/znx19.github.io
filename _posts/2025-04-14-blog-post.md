## Set up
- <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle (c,E) &#92;in &#92;mathcal{E}^{N}$</span> is a non-coalitional N-agent game with claim point <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle c$</span> and total resource <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle E$</span> such that <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N}c&#95;{i}&#92;geq E$</span>. An example is a bankrupt problem.
- Define the worth of <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle S &#92;subset N$</span> as <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle v&#92;left( c,S &#92;right):=&#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;}$</span>, then we get a cooperative game <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle v&#92;left( c,E &#92;right)=&#92;left( v&#92;left( c,S &#92;right) &#92;right)&#95;{S &#92;subset N}$</span>
## The cooperative game <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle &#92;displaystyle v&#92;left( c,E &#92;right)=&#92;left( v&#92;left( c,S &#92;right) &#92;right)&#95;{S &#92;subset N}$</span> is convex: <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle &#92;forall S,T&#92;subset N, v&#92;left( c,S&#92;cap T &#92;right)+v&#92;left( c,S&#92;cup T &#92;right)&#92;geq v&#92;left( c,S &#92;right)+v&#92;left( c,T &#92;right)$</span>

For any 2 subsets of <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle N$</span> : <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle S,T &#92;subset N$</span>
<div class="math">$$
&#92;begin{align}
& v&#92;left( S &#92;cup T &#92;right) - v&#92;left( S &#92;right)  =  &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in &#92;left( N-S &#92;right)&#92;cap&#92;left( N-T &#92;right)}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} - &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S}ci &#92;right&#92;} &#92;&#92;
& = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in &#92;left( N-S &#92;right)&#92;cap&#92;left( N-T &#92;right)}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} + &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,-E+&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S}ci &#92;right&#92;}  &#92;&#92;
& = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0+ &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,-E+&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S}ci &#92;right&#92;},E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in &#92;left( N-S &#92;right)&#92;cap&#92;left( N-T &#92;right)}c&#95;{i} + &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,-E+&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S}ci &#92;right&#92;}&#92;right&#92;}  &#92;&#92;
& = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,-E+&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S}ci &#92;right&#92;},&#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in &#92;left( N-S &#92;right)&#92;cap&#92;left( N-T &#92;right)}c&#95;{i}, &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in T-S}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;&#92;
& =: &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,x-a &#92;right&#92;},&#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ a, x &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;&#92;
& = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,a &#92;right&#92;} &#92;mathbb{I}&#95;{&#92;left&#92;{ x&#92;geq a &#92;right&#92;}} + &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ x-a,x &#92;right&#92;} &#92;mathbb{I}&#95;{&#92;left&#92;{ x< a &#92;right&#92;}} &#92;&#92;
& = a &#92;mathbb{I}&#95;{&#92;left&#92;{ x&#92;geq a&#92;geq 0 &#92;right&#92;}} + x&#92;mathbb{I}&#95;{&#92;left&#92;{ x< a &#92;right&#92;}}  &#92;&#92;
& = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,&#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ a,x &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right&#92;}
&#92;end{align}
$$</div>
And 
<div class="math">$$
&#92;begin{align}
& v&#92;left( T &#92;right)-V&#92;left( S&#92;cap T &#92;right) = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-T}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} - &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in &#92;left( N-S &#92;right)&#92;cup&#92;left( N-T &#92;right)}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;&#92;
 & = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-T}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} + &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,-E+&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in &#92;left( N-S &#92;right)&#92;cup&#92;left( N-T &#92;right)}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;&#92; 
 & = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0+ &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,-E+&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in &#92;left( N-S &#92;right)&#92;cup&#92;left( N-T &#92;right)}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;},E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-T}c&#95;{i}+ &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,-E+&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in &#92;left( N-S &#92;right)&#92;cup&#92;left( N-T &#92;right)}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right&#92;}  &#92;&#92;
& = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,-E+&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in &#92;left( N-S &#92;right)&#92;cup&#92;left( N-T&#95;{3} &#92;right)}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;},&#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-T}c&#95;{i},&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in T-S }c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right&#92;}  &#92;&#92; &#92;&#92;
& =: &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,x-b &#92;right&#92;},&#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ b, x &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;text{ where } x = &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in T-S }c&#95;{i}&#92;geq 0 &#92;&#92;
& = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,b &#92;right&#92;} &#92;mathbb{I}&#95;{&#92;left&#92;{ x&#92;geq b &#92;right&#92;}} + &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ x-b,x &#92;right&#92;} &#92;mathbb{I}&#95;{&#92;left&#92;{ x< b &#92;right&#92;}} &#92;&#92;
& = b &#92;mathbb{I}&#95;{&#92;left&#92;{ x&#92;geq b&#92;geq 0 &#92;right&#92;}} + x&#92;mathbb{I}&#95;{&#92;left&#92;{ x< b &#92;right&#92;}}  &#92;&#92;
& = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,&#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ b,x &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right&#92;}
&#92;end{align}
$$</div>
where 
<div class="math">$$
a=E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in &#92;left( N-S &#92;right)&#92;cap&#92;left( N-T &#92;right)}c&#95;{i} &#92;geq E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-T}c&#95;{i}=b
$$</div>
Finally we have the following
<div class="math">$$
&#92;begin{align}
& v&#92;left( S &#92;cup T &#92;right) - v&#92;left( S &#92;right)  = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,&#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ a,x &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;geq &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,&#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ b,x &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right&#92;} = v&#92;left( T &#92;right)-V&#92;left( S&#92;cap T &#92;right)
&#92;end{align}
$$</div>
## The core of this game is the collection of totally assigned allocations within the box of claim <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle C&#92;left( c&#92;left( c,v &#92;right) &#92;right)=&#92;left&#92;{ &#92;phi &#92;in &#92;mathbb{R}&#95;{+}^{N} : &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N}&#92;phi&#95;{i}=E, &#92;phi&#95;{i}&#92;leq c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;}$</span>
Definition of a core:
<div class="math">$$
&#92;begin{align}
C&#92;left( v&#92;left( c,E &#92;right) &#92;right)  :=  &#92;left&#92;{ &#92;phi &#92;in &#92;mathbb{R}&#95;{+}^{N}: &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N}&#92;phi&#95;{i}=E  &#92;cap   &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in S}&#92;phi&#95;{i}&#92;geq v&#92;left( c,S &#92;right) &#92;forall S&#92;subset N &#92;right&#92;}
&#92;end{align}
$$</div>
### Step 1: The core of such game is nonempty
Given the construction of value <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle v&#92;left( c,S &#92;right):==&#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;}$</span>, we have the following
<div class="math">$$
&#92;begin{align}
&#92;sum&#95;{i}&#92;phi&#95;{i}=E  &  & &#92;implies   & & &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in S}&#92;phi&#95;{i}&#92;geq v&#92;left( c,S &#92;right)  &  & &#92;forall S &#92;subset N
&#92;end{align}
$$</div>
Consider any balanced subset pair <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle (S&#95;{1},S&#95;{2}&#92;dots,S&#95;{K}) (r&#95;{1}, r&#95;{2}&#92;dots,r&#95;{K})$</span> with each <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle S&#95;{k} &#92;subset N$</span>, <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle r&#95;{k} &#92;geq 0$</span>, we have
<div class="math">$$
&#92;begin{align}
 & r&#95;{1}S&#95;{1}+r&#95;{2}S&#95;{2}+&#92;dots +r&#95;{K}S&#95;{K}=N &#92;&#92;
&#92;implies & r&#95;{1}&#92;left( N-S&#95;{1} &#92;right)+r&#95;{2}&#92;left( N-S&#95;{2} &#92;right) + &#92;dots+r&#95;{K}&#92;left( N-S&#95;{K} &#92;right)=&#92;sum&#95;{k=1}^{K}r&#95;{k} N-N=&#92;left( &#92;sum&#95;{k}r&#95;{k}-1 &#92;right)N
&#92;end{align}
$$</div>
Notice that the combination of <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle (N-S&#95;{k})&#95;{k}$</span> with weight <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle (r&#95;{k})$</span> should not be negative, i.e. <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle &#92;sum&#95;{k}r&#95;{k}-1&#92;geq 0$</span> .
Thus, for such a balanced subset pair:
<div class="math">$$
&#92;begin{align}
&#92;sum&#95;{k=1}^{K}r&#95;{k}v&#92;left( c,S&#95;{k} &#92;right) & =  &#92;sum&#95;{k=1}^{K}r&#95;{k}&#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S&#95;{k}c&#95;{i}} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;&#92;
& = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;limits&#95;{&#92;kappa &#92;subset &#92;left&#92;{ 1, 2, &#92;dots,K &#92;right&#92;}}&#92;left&#92;{ &#92;sum&#95;{k &#92;in &#92;kappa}r&#95;{k}E- &#92;sum&#95;{k &#92;in &#92;kappa}r&#95;{k}&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S&#95;{k}}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;&#92;
& = &#92;sum&#95;{k &#92;in Q}r&#95;{k}E-&#92;sum&#95;{k &#92;in Q}r&#95;{k}&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S&#95;{k}}c&#95;{i} &#92;&#92;
& &#92;text{where  } Q:=&#92;left&#92;{ k&#92;in&#92;left&#92;{ 1, 2,&#92;dots K &#92;right&#92;}:E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S&#95;{k}}c&#95;{i}&#92;geq 0 &#92;right&#92;}
&#92;end{align}
$$</div>
If <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle Q=&#92;left&#92;{ 1, 2, &#92;dots,K &#92;right&#92;}$</span>, the value of <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle v&#92;left( N &#92;right)=E$</span> satisfies
<div class="math">$$
v&#92;left( N &#92;right)=E&#92;geq &#92;sum&#95;{k}r&#95;{k}v&#92;left( c,S&#95;{k} &#92;right)=&#92;sum&#95;{k}r&#95;{k}E-&#92;left( &#92;sum&#95;{k}r&#95;{k}-1 &#92;right)&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N} c&#95;{i}
$$</div>
since <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in I}c&#95;{i}&#92;geq E$</span> and <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle &#92;sum&#95;{k}r&#95;{k}-1&#92;geq 0$</span>.
If <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle Q &#92;subset &#92;{ 1, 2, &#92;dots,K &#92;}$</span>, consider a "shrinking" of claim from point <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle c$</span> to <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle c'$</span>, such that <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle c'=c+&#92;Delta c$</span>  with <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle &#92;Delta c&#92;geq 0$</span>, <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N}c'&#95;{i}&#92;geq E$</span> and <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle Q'=&#92;left&#92;{ 1, 2, &#92;dots K &#92;right&#92;}$</span>. Thus, the following holds
<div class="math">$$
&#92;begin{align}
v&#92;left( N &#92;right)=E & &#92;geq &#92;sum&#95;{k &#92;in Q'=&#92;left&#92;{ 1, 2,&#92;dots K &#92;right&#92;}}r&#95;{k}v&#92;left( c',S&#95;{k} &#92;right) &#92;geq &#92;sum&#95;{k &#92;in Q}r&#95;{k}v&#92;left( c',S&#95;{k} &#92;right) &#92;&#92;
 & = &#92;sum&#95;{k &#92;in Q}r&#95;{k}E-&#92;left( &#92;sum&#95;{k}r&#95;{k}-1 &#92;right)&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S&#95;{k}}c'&#95;{i} &#92;geq &#92;sum&#95;{k &#92;in Q}r&#95;{k}E-&#92;left( &#92;sum&#95;{k}r&#95;{k}-1 &#92;right)&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S&#95;{k}}c'&#95;{i} &#92;&#92;
 & = &#92;sum&#95;{k &#92;in Q} r&#95;{k}v&#92;left( c,S&#95;{k} &#92;right)
&#92;end{align}
$$</div>
where the first inequality comes from the case when <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle Q'=&#92;left&#92;{ 1, 2, &#92;dots K &#92;right&#92;}$</span>.
As a conclusion, given <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in I}&#92;phi&#95;{i}=E$</span>, any balanced subset pair satisfies the non-binding condition <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle v&#92;left( N &#92;right)&#92;geq &#92;sum&#95;{k} r&#95;{k}v&#92;left( c,S&#95;{k} &#92;right)$</span>. From Shapley's conclusion, <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in S}&#92;phi&#95;{i}&#92;geq v&#92;left( c,S &#92;right)$</span> is satisfied for all <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle S &#92;subset N$</span>. Thus, the core to this game is non-empty .
### Step 2: the core is <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle C&#92;left( v&#92;left( c,S &#92;right) &#92;right)=&#92;left&#92;{ &#92;phi &#92;in &#92;mathbb{R}&#95;{+}^{N}: &#92;sum&#95;{i}&#92;phi&#95;{i}=E &#92;cap &#92;phi&#95;{i} &#92;leq c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;}$</span>
For any subset of agents <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle S &#92;subset N$</span>:
<div class="math">$$
&#92;begin{align}
 & &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in S} &#92;phi&#95;{i} &#92;geq &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;not&#92;in S}c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;&#92;
&#92;Leftrightarrow  & E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;not&#92;in S} &#92;phi&#95;{i} &#92;geq E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;not&#92;in S}c&#95;{i}  & &#92;cap &  &  E-&#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;not&#92;in S} c&#95;{i}&#92;geq 0 &#92;&#92;
&#92;Leftrightarrow  & &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S} c&#95;{i} &#92;geq &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N-S} &#92;phi&#95;{i}&#92;geq 0 &#92;&#92;
&#92;Leftrightarrow  & &#92;phi&#95;{i} &#92;in [0,c&#95;{i}]
&#92;end{align}
$$</div>

## The Shapley value of the cooperative game is the random arrival awards vector of the non-cooperative game <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle Sh&#92;left( v&#92;left( c,E &#92;right) &#92;right)=RA&#92;left( c,E &#92;right)$</span>
For the exact endowment case with <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N}c&#95;{i}=E$</span>, it is obvious that <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle Sh&#92;left( v &#92;right)&#95;{i}=RA&#92;left( c,E &#92;right)&#95;{i}=c&#95;{i}$</span>.
For the insufficient endowment case with <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle &#92;sum&#95;{i &#92;in N}c&#95;{i}<M$</span>, consider the following 2 orders:
If agent <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle i$</span> arrives after the set of other agents <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle S &#92;subset N$</span>, the Shapley value is the following:
<div class="math">$$
&#92;begin{align}
Sh&#95;{i}&#92;left( S,i, &#92;dots &#92;right) & =v&#92;left( c,S&#92;cup&#92;left&#92;{ i &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right)-v&#92;left( c,S &#92;right)=&#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,E-&#92;sum&#95;{j &#92;in N-S-i}c&#95;{j} &#92;right&#92;}-&#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,E-&#92;sum&#95;{j &#92;in N-S}c&#95;{j} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;&#92;
 & = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,E-&#92;sum&#95;{j &#92;in N-S-i}c&#95;{j} &#92;right&#92;}+&#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,-E+&#92;sum&#95;{j &#92;in N-S}c&#95;{j} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;&#92;
 & = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,-E+&#92;sum&#95;{j &#92;in N-S}c&#95;{j} &#92;right&#92;},E-&#92;sum&#95;{j &#92;in N-S-i}c&#95;{j} + &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,-E+&#92;sum&#95;{j &#92;in N-S}c&#95;{j} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;&#92;
 & = &#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,-E+&#92;sum&#95;{j &#92;in N-S}c&#95;{j} &#92;right&#92;}, &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ E-&#92;sum&#95;{j &#92;in N-S-i}c&#95;{j}, c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;&#92;
 & =&#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{ 0,&#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ E-&#92;sum&#95;{j &#92;in N-S-i}c&#95;{j}, c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right&#92;}
&#92;end{align}
$$</div>
If agent <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle i$</span> arrives before the set of other agents <span class="math">$&#92;displaystyle S &#92;subset N$</span>, the random arrival value is the following:
<div class="math">$$
RA&#95;{i}&#92;left( &#92;dots, i, S &#92;right) =&#92;mathop{&#92;max}&#92;left&#92;{0, &#92;mathop{&#92;min}&#92;left&#92;{ E-&#92;sum&#95;{j &#92;in N-S-i}c&#95;{j},c&#95;{i} &#92;right&#92;} &#92;right&#92;}
$$</div>
Thus, the Shapley value and random arrival value seem to be symmetric on order of individuals, and the weighted average of values on order is the same.
<div class="math">$$
Sh&#95;{i}=&#92;sum&#95;{S&#92;subset N} &#92;frac{|S|! |N-S-1|!}{N!}Sh&#95;{i}&#92;left( S,i, &#92;dots &#92;right) = &#92;sum&#95;{S&#92;subset N} &#92;frac{|N-S-1|! |S|!}{N!}RA&#95;{i}&#92;left( &#92;dots,i,S &#92;right) = RA&#95;{i}
$$</div>
