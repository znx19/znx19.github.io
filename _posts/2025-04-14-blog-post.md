## Set up
- $\displaystyle (c,E) \in \mathcal{E}^{N}$ is a non-coalitional N-agent game with claim point $\displaystyle c$ and total resource $\displaystyle E$ such that $\displaystyle \sum_{i \in N}c_{i}\geq E$. An example is a bankrupt problem.
- Define the worth of $\displaystyle S \subset N$ as $\displaystyle v\left( c,S \right):=\mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,E-\sum_{i \in N-S}c_{i} \right\}$, then we get a cooperative game $\displaystyle v\left( c,E \right)=\left( v\left( c,S \right) \right)_{S \subset N}$
## The cooperative game $\displaystyle \displaystyle v\left( c,E \right)=\left( v\left( c,S \right) \right)_{S \subset N}$ is convex: $\displaystyle \forall S,T\subset N, v\left( c,S\cap T \right)+v\left( c,S\cup T \right)\geq v\left( c,S \right)+v\left( c,T \right)$

For any 2 subsets of $\displaystyle N$ : $\displaystyle S,T \subset N$
$$
\begin{align}
& v\left( S \cup T \right) - v\left( S \right)  =  \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,E-\sum_{i \in \left( N-S \right)\cap\left( N-T \right)}c_{i} \right\} - \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,E-\sum_{i \in N-S}ci \right\} \\
& = \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,E-\sum_{i \in \left( N-S \right)\cap\left( N-T \right)}c_{i} \right\} + \mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,-E+\sum_{i \in N-S}ci \right\}  \\
& = \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0+ \mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,-E+\sum_{i \in N-S}ci \right\},E-\sum_{i \in \left( N-S \right)\cap\left( N-T \right)}c_{i} + \mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,-E+\sum_{i \in N-S}ci \right\}\right\}  \\
& = \mathop{\max}\left\{ \mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,-E+\sum_{i \in N-S}ci \right\},\mathop{\min}\left\{ E-\sum_{i \in \left( N-S \right)\cap\left( N-T \right)}c_{i}, \sum_{i \in T-S}c_{i} \right\} \right\} \\
& =: \mathop{\max}\left\{ \mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,x-a \right\},\mathop{\min}\left\{ a, x \right\} \right\} \\
& = \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,a \right\} \mathbb{I}_{\left\{ x\geq a \right\}} + \mathop{\max}\left\{ x-a,x \right\} \mathbb{I}_{\left\{ x< a \right\}} \\
& = a \mathbb{I}_{\left\{ x\geq a\geq 0 \right\}} + x\mathbb{I}_{\left\{ x< a \right\}}  \\
& = \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,\mathop{\min}\left\{ a,x \right\} \right\}
\end{align}
$$
And 
$$
\begin{align}
& v\left( T \right)-V\left( S\cap T \right) = \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,E-\sum_{i \in N-T}c_{i} \right\} - \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,E-\sum_{i \in \left( N-S \right)\cup\left( N-T \right)}c_{i} \right\} \\
 & = \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,E-\sum_{i \in N-T}c_{i} \right\} + \mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,-E+\sum_{i \in \left( N-S \right)\cup\left( N-T \right)}c_{i} \right\} \\ 
 & = \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0+ \mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,-E+\sum_{i \in \left( N-S \right)\cup\left( N-T \right)}c_{i} \right\},E-\sum_{i \in N-T}c_{i}+ \mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,-E+\sum_{i \in \left( N-S \right)\cup\left( N-T \right)}c_{i} \right\} \right\}  \\
& = \mathop{\max}\left\{ \mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,-E+\sum_{i \in \left( N-S \right)\cup\left( N-T_{3} \right)}c_{i} \right\},\mathop{\min}\left\{ E-\sum_{i \in N-T}c_{i},\sum_{i \in T-S }c_{i} \right\} \right\}  \\ \\
& =: \mathop{\max}\left\{ \mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,x-b \right\},\mathop{\min}\left\{ b, x \right\} \right\} \text{ where } x = \sum_{i \in T-S }c_{i}\geq 0 \\
& = \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,b \right\} \mathbb{I}_{\left\{ x\geq b \right\}} + \mathop{\max}\left\{ x-b,x \right\} \mathbb{I}_{\left\{ x< b \right\}} \\
& = b \mathbb{I}_{\left\{ x\geq b\geq 0 \right\}} + x\mathbb{I}_{\left\{ x< b \right\}}  \\
& = \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,\mathop{\min}\left\{ b,x \right\} \right\}
\end{align}
$$
where 
$$
a=E-\sum_{i \in \left( N-S \right)\cap\left( N-T \right)}c_{i} \geq E-\sum_{i \in N-T}c_{i}=b
$$
Finally we have the following
$$
\begin{align}
& v\left( S \cup T \right) - v\left( S \right)  = \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,\mathop{\min}\left\{ a,x \right\} \right\} \geq \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,\mathop{\min}\left\{ b,x \right\} \right\} = v\left( T \right)-V\left( S\cap T \right)
\end{align}
$$
## The core of this game is the collection of totally assigned allocations within the box of claim $\displaystyle C\left( c\left( c,v \right) \right)=\left\{ \phi \in \mathbb{R}_{+}^{N} : \sum_{i \in N}\phi_{i}=E, \phi_{i}\leq c_{i} \right\}$
Definition of a core:
$$
\begin{align}
C\left( v\left( c,E \right) \right)  :=  \left\{ \phi \in \mathbb{R}_{+}^{N}: \sum_{i \in N}\phi_{i}=E  \cap   \sum_{i \in S}\phi_{i}\geq v\left( c,S \right) \forall S\subset N \right\}
\end{align}
$$
### Step 1: The core of such game is nonempty
Given the construction of value $\displaystyle v\left( c,S \right):==\mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,E-\sum_{i \in N-S}c_{i} \right\}$, we have the following
$$
\begin{align}
\sum_{i}\phi_{i}=E  &  & \implies   & & \sum_{i \in S}\phi_{i}\geq v\left( c,S \right)  &  & \forall S \subset N
\end{align}
$$
Consider any balanced subset pair $\displaystyle (S_{1},S_{2}\dots,S_{K}) (r_{1}, r_{2}\dots,r_{K})$ with each $\displaystyle S_{k} \subset N$, $\displaystyle r_{k} \geq 0$, we have
$$
\begin{align}
 & r_{1}S_{1}+r_{2}S_{2}+\dots +r_{K}S_{K}=N \\
\implies & r_{1}\left( N-S_{1} \right)+r_{2}\left( N-S_{2} \right) + \dots+r_{K}\left( N-S_{K} \right)=\sum_{k=1}^{K}r_{k} N-N=\left( \sum_{k}r_{k}-1 \right)N
\end{align}
$$
Notice that the combination of $\displaystyle (N-S_{k})_{k}$ with weight $\displaystyle (r_{k})$ should not be negative, i.e. $\displaystyle \sum_{k}r_{k}-1\geq 0$ .
Thus, for such a balanced subset pair:
$$
\begin{align}
\sum_{k=1}^{K}r_{k}v\left( c,S_{k} \right) & =  \sum_{k=1}^{K}r_{k}\mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,E-\sum_{i \in N-S_{k}c_{i}} \right\} \\
& = \mathop{\max}\limits_{\kappa \subset \left\{ 1, 2, \dots,K \right\}}\left\{ \sum_{k \in \kappa}r_{k}E- \sum_{k \in \kappa}r_{k}\sum_{i \in N-S_{k}}c_{i} \right\} \\
& = \sum_{k \in Q}r_{k}E-\sum_{k \in Q}r_{k}\sum_{i \in N-S_{k}}c_{i} \\
& \text{where  } Q:=\left\{ k\in\left\{ 1, 2,\dots K \right\}:E-\sum_{i \in N-S_{k}}c_{i}\geq 0 \right\}
\end{align}
$$
If $\displaystyle Q=\left\{ 1, 2, \dots,K \right\}$, the value of $\displaystyle v\left( N \right)=E$ satisfies
$$
v\left( N \right)=E\geq \sum_{k}r_{k}v\left( c,S_{k} \right)=\sum_{k}r_{k}E-\left( \sum_{k}r_{k}-1 \right)\sum_{i \in N} c_{i}
$$
since $\displaystyle \sum_{i \in I}c_{i}\geq E$ and $\displaystyle \sum_{k}r_{k}-1\geq 0$.
If $\displaystyle Q \subset \{ 1, 2, \dots,K \}$, consider a "shrinking" of claim from point $\displaystyle c$ to $\displaystyle c'$, such that $\displaystyle c'=c+\Delta c$  with $\displaystyle \Delta c\geq 0$, $\displaystyle \sum_{i \in N}c'_{i}\geq E$ and $\displaystyle Q'=\left\{ 1, 2, \dots K \right\}$. Thus, the following holds
$$
\begin{align}
v\left( N \right)=E & \geq \sum_{k \in Q'=\left\{ 1, 2,\dots K \right\}}r_{k}v\left( c',S_{k} \right) \geq \sum_{k \in Q}r_{k}v\left( c',S_{k} \right) \\
 & = \sum_{k \in Q}r_{k}E-\left( \sum_{k}r_{k}-1 \right)\sum_{i \in N-S_{k}}c'_{i} \geq \sum_{k \in Q}r_{k}E-\left( \sum_{k}r_{k}-1 \right)\sum_{i \in N-S_{k}}c'_{i} \\
 & = \sum_{k \in Q} r_{k}v\left( c,S_{k} \right)
\end{align}
$$
where the first inequality comes from the case when $\displaystyle Q'=\left\{ 1, 2, \dots K \right\}$.
As a conclusion, given $\displaystyle \sum_{i \in I}\phi_{i}=E$, any balanced subset pair satisfies the non-binding condition $\displaystyle v\left( N \right)\geq \sum_{k} r_{k}v\left( c,S_{k} \right)$. From Shapley's conclusion, $\displaystyle \sum_{i \in S}\phi_{i}\geq v\left( c,S \right)$ is satisfied for all $\displaystyle S \subset N$. Thus, the core to this game is non-empty .
### Step 2: the core is $\displaystyle C\left( v\left( c,S \right) \right)=\left\{ \phi \in \mathbb{R}_{+}^{N}: \sum_{i}\phi_{i}=E \cap \phi_{i} \leq c_{i} \right\}$
For any subset of agents $\displaystyle S \subset N$:
$$
\begin{align}
 & \sum_{i \in S} \phi_{i} \geq \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,E-\sum_{i \not\in S}c_{i} \right\} \\
\Leftrightarrow  & E-\sum_{i \not\in S} \phi_{i} \geq E-\sum_{i \not\in S}c_{i}  & \cap &  &  E-\sum_{i \not\in S} c_{i}\geq 0 \\
\Leftrightarrow  & \sum_{i \in N-S} c_{i} \geq \sum_{i \in N-S} \phi_{i}\geq 0 \\
\Leftrightarrow  & \phi_{i} \in [0,c_{i}]
\end{align}
$$

## The Shapley value of the cooperative game is the random arrival awards vector of the non-cooperative game $\displaystyle Sh\left( v\left( c,E \right) \right)=RA\left( c,E \right)$
For the exact endowment case with $\displaystyle \sum_{i \in N}c_{i}=E$, it is obvious that $\displaystyle Sh\left( v \right)_{i}=RA\left( c,E \right)_{i}=c_{i}$.
For the insufficient endowment case with $\displaystyle \sum_{i \in N}c_{i}<M$, consider the following 2 orders:
If agent $\displaystyle i$ arrives after the set of other agents $\displaystyle S \subset N$, the Shapley value is the following:
$$
\begin{align}
Sh_{i}\left( S,i, \dots \right) & =v\left( c,S\cup\left\{ i \right\} \right)-v\left( c,S \right)=\mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,E-\sum_{j \in N-S-i}c_{j} \right\}-\mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,E-\sum_{j \in N-S}c_{j} \right\} \\
 & = \mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,E-\sum_{j \in N-S-i}c_{j} \right\}+\mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,-E+\sum_{j \in N-S}c_{j} \right\} \\
 & = \mathop{\max}\left\{ \mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,-E+\sum_{j \in N-S}c_{j} \right\},E-\sum_{j \in N-S-i}c_{j} + \mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,-E+\sum_{j \in N-S}c_{j} \right\} \right\} \\
 & = \mathop{\max}\left\{ \mathop{\min}\left\{ 0,-E+\sum_{j \in N-S}c_{j} \right\}, \mathop{\min}\left\{ E-\sum_{j \in N-S-i}c_{j}, c_{i} \right\} \right\} \\
 & =\mathop{\max}\left\{ 0,\mathop{\min}\left\{ E-\sum_{j \in N-S-i}c_{j}, c_{i} \right\} \right\}
\end{align}
$$
If agent $\displaystyle i$ arrives before the set of other agents $\displaystyle S \subset N$, the random arrival value is the following:
$$
RA_{i}\left( \dots, i, S \right) =\mathop{\max}\left\{0, \mathop{\min}\left\{ E-\sum_{j \in N-S-i}c_{j},c_{i} \right\} \right\}
$$
Thus, the Shapley value and random arrival value seem to be symmetric on order of individuals, and the weighted average of values on order is the same.
$$
Sh_{i}=\sum_{S\subset N} \frac{|S|! |N-S-1|!}{N!}Sh_{i}\left( S,i, \dots \right) = \sum_{S\subset N} \frac{|N-S-1|! |S|!}{N!}RA_{i}\left( \dots,i,S \right) = RA_{i}
$$
